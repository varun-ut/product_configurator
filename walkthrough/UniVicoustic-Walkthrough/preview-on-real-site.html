This page gives you two ways to run the 24-step tour against the live configurator.univicoustic.com — without your developer having to deploy anything yet.

Why didn't the other demo look right? Browsers block websites from being embedded inside another page for security. The earlier univicoustic-tour-demo.html is a simplified mockup of the layout so you can see the tour mechanics. To see it on the real UI, use one of the methods below.

Option 2 — DevTools console (for developers)

  1. Open the configurator in a browser tab.
  2. Press F12 (Windows) or Cmd + Option + I (Mac) to open DevTools, and click the Console tab.
  3. Paste the one-liner below and hit Enter: 
    var s=document.createElement('script');s.src='https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/shepherd.js@12.0.0/dist/js/shepherd.min.js';var c=document.createElement('link');c.rel='stylesheet';c.href='https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/shepherd.js@12.0.0/dist/css/shepherd.css';document.head.appendChild(c);s.onload=function(){var t=new Shepherd.Tour({useModalOverlay:true,defaultStepOptions:{scrollTo:true,cancelIcon:{enabled:true},arrow:true}});t.addStep({title:'UniVicoustic walkthrough',text:'Tour loaded.   4. A mini test tooltip appears. For the full 24-step tour, paste the contents of univicoustic-tour.js into the console instead.

Option 3 — Deploy it (for your developer)

Once you're happy with the tour, have your developer add this line just before </body> in the configurator's main template:

<script src="/assets/univicoustic-tour.js" defer></script>

…using the univicoustic-tour.js file in this folder. The tour will auto-launch for every first-time visitor and skip thereafter.

Tip: The bookmarklet stays on your bookmarks bar forever — you can click it on any page-refresh or session to replay the tour while reviewing. It doesn't save anything to the actual site.
